When a plan stops being a plan
Three endings, one shape. A plan leaves the live app by exactly one door — the circle's Past section — and the only difference between called off, didn't happen, and happened is one word on the card. The rules I'm proposing: Plans only ever holds things that still need you, so all three drop out of the feed the moment they end; the one exception is a cancellation of a plan you'd said yes to, which earns a single dismissable notice because you were counting on it. Reopen lives on the host's cancelled detail screen only, and only while the date is still ahead — after the date there is nothing to reopen. And for a plan that simply happened: nothing changes for MVP — no recap, no photos, no attendance. It greys out and files itself.
19a
Called off — what everyone sees
The slot bar goes, entirely — a count is a question, and there's no question left. In its place a flat stone card carrying the two facts that matter: it's off, and why. The footer empties and is removed, not disabled: a greyed-out "I'm in" is a button lying about being tappable. Nothing replaces it either — the cancellation already pushed to everyone involved, so there's no message to send. The screen is now purely a record.
